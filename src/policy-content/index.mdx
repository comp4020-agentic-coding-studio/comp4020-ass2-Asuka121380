---

<div className="t-rule pol-section" id="late-work">
  <h2 className="t-h2">Late work and extensions</h2>
</div>
<p className="t-body">
  If something outside your control is going to make a due date
  genuinely unworkable, get in touch with the relevant
  assessment's marker before the deadline, not after it.
</p>
<div className="pol-callout">
  A short, specific extension request made early is easy to
  grant; the same request made after the deadline has already
  passed is not.
</div>

<div className="t-rule pol-section" id="academic-integrity">
  <h2 className="t-h2">Academic integrity</h2>
</div>
<p className="t-body">
  Analysis, predictions, and design justifications should be your
  own reasoning about the specific sample or target you were
  given. Discussing concepts with classmates is expected and
  encouraged — the weekly teaching sessions are built around
  exactly that.
</p>
<div className="pol-callout">
  The written reasoning you submit should be traceable to your
  own understanding of it.
</div>

<div className="t-rule pol-section" id="getting-help">
  <h2 className="t-h2">Getting help</h2>
</div>
<p className="t-body">
  For a question about a specific piece of work in progress,
  bring it to your next teaching session rather than emailing it
  — that's where a tutor can look at what you're seeing
  alongside you. For anything about the course itself rather
  than a specific piece of work, see <a href="../people/">People</a> for who to contact
  and how.
</p>
