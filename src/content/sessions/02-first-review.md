## Before the session

Bring a short recorded sound — a guitar note is ideal, but any single
sustained pitch works for this session. Have a rough guess ready for what
its waveform and spectrum will look like before you see them.

## In the session

Everyone's sample gets run through the same waveform and spectrum view used
in the Week 1-4 lectures. The session is feedback-first: for each sample, the
group says what it can actually see in the plot, then whether that matches
the guess made beforehand. The goal is practice at telling an observation
apart from an interpretation — the exact distinction the
[Tone Autopsy](/assessments/) assessment will ask you to make explicitly,
several weeks later.

## Afterwards

This session isn't marked, but it's the closest rehearsal you'll get for Tone
Autopsy before it's due. If a claim about your sample didn't hold up under
scrutiny here, that's exactly the kind of claim worth re-checking before you
submit.
