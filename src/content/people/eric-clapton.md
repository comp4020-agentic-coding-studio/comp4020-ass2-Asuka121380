Eric tutors the weeks where a signal's frequency content and gain get
reshaped downstream of the guitar itself: a pedal's control cavity, tone
filtering, and an amplifier's own staged gain and tone stack. Eric is the
person most often fielding "why does the same knob sound different in a
different position in the chain" questions, because the honest answer is
usually "it depends what's already happened to the signal by then."

Eric's own background is in acoustics, which comes through in how the bench
sessions frame a filter or a tone stack — as a frequency-response curve
first, a "sound" second.

**Consultation:** ask during a session first — most questions about a
control on the bench are faster to resolve by looking at it together than by
describing it in an email. Email for anything that can't wait until the next
session, or that needs a considered written answer.
