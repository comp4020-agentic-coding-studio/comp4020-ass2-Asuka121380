Marisol designed this course around a single premise: that "guitar tone" is
usually discussed as a matter of taste when most of it is actually a matter
of physics and signal processing that can be taught, measured, and reasoned
about directly. The twelve-week structure — string, pickup, gain stage,
clipping, filter, amplifier, cabinet, and the ear, in that order — is
Marisol's answer to "what would it look like to teach tone as engineering?"

As convenor, Marisol owns the shape of the course and each week's central
question, and is the person to escalate to if something about the course
itself — not a specific piece of work — isn't landing.

**Consultation:** email for course-level questions (structure, assessment
weighting, anything that affects more than your own work). Feedback on your
own work in progress belongs in the weekly session, with the tutor running
it — that's where it gets seen alongside the concept it's building on.
