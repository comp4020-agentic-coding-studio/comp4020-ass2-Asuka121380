John tutors the weeks where a signal first gets amplified: the string and
fretboard's fixed physical properties, and the gain stages that later push a
clean signal toward its headroom. Questions about why a control cavity is
wired the way it is, or why a gain stage clips before another one does, are
the ones John is most often asked on the bench.

John's own background is in instrumentation and measurement, which shapes
how the bench sessions run — a claim about a control's effect gets checked
against a meter or a plot before it gets accepted as fact.

**Consultation:** ask during a session first — most questions about a
control on the bench are faster to resolve by looking at it together than by
describing it in an email. Email for anything that can't wait until the next
session, or that needs a considered written answer.
