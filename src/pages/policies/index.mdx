# Policies and support

## Assessment progression

The three assessments follow the semester in order — Analyse, then
Manipulate, then Design — and each one only assumes material the course has
actually covered by its due date. [Tone Autopsy](/assessments/) draws only
on Weeks 1-4; you will not be expected to explain distortion or amplifier
behaviour there, because those come later.

## Late work and extensions

If something outside your control is going to make a due date genuinely
unworkable, get in touch with the relevant assessment's marker before the
deadline, not after it. A short, specific extension request made early is
easy to grant; the same request made after the deadline has already passed
is not.

## Academic integrity

Analysis, predictions, and design justifications should be your own
reasoning about the specific sample or target you were given. Discussing
concepts with classmates is expected and encouraged — the weekly sessions
are built around exactly that — but the written reasoning you submit should
be traceable to your own understanding of it.

## Getting help

For a question about a specific piece of work in progress, bring it to the
[weekly session](/sessions/) rather than emailing it — that's where a tutor
can look at what you're seeing alongside you. For anything about the course
itself rather than a specific piece of work, see [People](/people/) for who
to contact and how.
